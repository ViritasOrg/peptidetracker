## What Are AAS?

Anabolic androgenic steroids are all derivatives of the testosterone molecule, manipulated in various ways to accentuate some characteristics and deemphasize others. Typically taken by athletes in the competitive sphere, bodybuilders, physique athletes, and gym people who want supraphysiological muscle mass and sometimes supraphysiologically low body fat.

**Dose range:**
- TRT (physiologic replacement): 80-140mg/week. Peter Attia's median clinical dose: ~80mg/week; max given: 140mg/week
- Supraphysiological bodybuilding use: 250mg/week up to 1,000mg/week (baseline test alone)
- Total compound load: commonly 800-1,600mg/week or more (multiple compounds)
- Mike Israetel's personal peak: just over 2,000mg/week; currently on 250mg/week

## Most Common Testosterone Esters

- **Enanthate & Cypionate** — by far most commonly used (inject 1-2x/week)
- **Propionate** — shorter ester, some prefer daily injections
- Daily injections provide the smoothest hormone curve

## Psychological Effects of High Testosterone

Moving into "enhanced male" territory — more masculine psychological patterns on average:

- Quieter, fewer facial expressions of emotion
- Less empathy, reduced ability to fine-tune others' emotions
- More irritable, more prone to anger/aggressive thoughts
- Heightened dominance hierarchy awareness — takes slights more personally
- Linguistically less expressive, reduced communication fluidity (damaging for relationships)
- Increased sex drive (quantitative and qualitative) — especially with high estrogen co-present
- Highly individual variation — some people have panic attacks, some feel almost nothing

Mike's note: "Roid rage is real" — but steroids don't accentuate every quality, just more masculine ones. Video on RP Strength channel covers this.

## Estrogen Management — The Modern "Safer Use" Model

**Key insight:** Aromatase inhibitors (AIs) are TOXIC — neurotoxic, cardiovascularly toxic. Used in breast cancer treatment (giant hammer for a small nail). Whoever runs the most AIs has done the worst to their health in the bodybuilding industry.

**Modern wisdom (evidence-based "safer use" crowd, e.g. Joe Jeffrey, UK):**
- Manage estrogen by controlling the *ratio* of estrogenically converting vs. non-converting compounds
- Do NOT use AIs routinely — only a few weeks pre-contest if needed
- Use Primobolan/Masteron instead of AIs to control estrogen levels

**Practical example:**
- 1,000mg test alone → high estrogen for some people
- Alternative: 500mg test + 500mg Primobolan → get estrogen from test but limit conversion, still high anabolism
- Ratios vary: 250 test / 750 Primo, 750 test / 250 Primo, anything in between
- Experiment based on how you *feel, look, and bloodwork* at various ratios

**High estrogen — individual responses vary enormously:**
- Some: amazing — joints feel great, recovery/sleep/sex drive/physique all excellent
- Others: water bloat, can't see abs at 8% BF, mood swings, poor sleep

**Low estrogen (from too much AI):**
- Complete loss of sex drive, don't even remember why sex exists

## Primobolan vs. Masteron

- **Primobolan (Metenolone)** — DHT derivative, doesn't aromatize, highly refined/specific, "pure protein accretion," predictable outcomes, most anabolic relative to androgenic (close to 10 on Mike's scale where 5=testosterone)
- **Masteron** — also non-aromatizing DHT derivative; actually *antagonizes* estrogen conversion from test to some extent; drying effect

## Nandrolone (Deca / NPP)

- More on the anabolic side than test
- Aromatizes into estrogen → provides intramuscular and intra-joint hydration; good for heavy training and injury prevention
- "Deca dick" — side effect: erectile dysfunction. Nandrolone interacts with progesterone receptor → horny but physically doesn't work
- For guys who are naturally very dry → nandrolone prevents joint issues during hard training cycles
- Interesting pharmacology in scalp: five-alpha reduces to dihydronandrolone (almost no androgenic activity) → can build muscle WITHOUT hair loss (unique among steroids)
- **NPP vs. Deca:** NPP clears in 3-5 days if you drop it; Deca lingers for weeks

## Trenbolone

- Classified as a "steroidal SARM" — prescribed to women in 1980s, used to beef up cattle
- Super anabolic + odd progestogenic activity (interacts with progesterone receptor)
- Side effects: severe night sweats ("Tren sweats"), "Tren cough" (severe coughing fit 20 seconds after injection — tightness in chest)
- More suppressive due to progesterone receptor interaction (negative feedback via AR, ER, AND PR)

## SARMs (Selective Androgen Receptor Modulators)

- Aim: tissue-selective anabolic activity with minimal androgenic effects
- Proportionally more selective than AAS, but *ceiling of anabolic activity is lower* — less muscle gain
- At higher doses, selectivity decreases (become less tissue-specific)
- Most common: LGD-4033 (Ligandrol / VK5211) — phase 2 trial for hip fractures; Ostarine (Enobosarm) — most well-known, not FDA approved; popular with women bridging to steroids
- All banned in natural bodybuilding (detectable as shouldn't be in body at all)
- Available via underground labs and some compounding pharmacies

## Androgen Receptor Saturation

- Mike's experience: gets almost the same gains at 1,000mg as at 2,000mg
- North of 1,500mg: mentally unstable ("insane") with no additional physique benefit for him
- Evidence that AR receptor *density escalates* with more androgen exposure (not down-regulates) → so theoretically more is more, but practically hits asymptote
- Individual variation is enormous

## Estrogen's Synergistic Role in Anabolism

- Estrogen alone: not very anabolic
- Estrogen *in presence of testosterone*: MORE anabolic than max testosterone without aromatization ability
- So baseline estrogen from test aromatization is actually desirable, not just a side effect to suppress
- Estrogen: cardioprotective, neuroprotective, increases strength, increases sex drive, increases anabolism in presence of androgens

## Starting Approach

Work with a knowledgeable coach:
1. Start very low doses of everything
2. Slowly introduce compounds
3. Note every beneficial and deleterious effect
4. Find a balance acceptable for long-term consequences
5. Don't rush — take a slow titration approach
