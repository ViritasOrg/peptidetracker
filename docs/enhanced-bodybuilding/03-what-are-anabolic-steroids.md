## What Are They?

**Anabolic Androgenic Steroids (AAS):**
- Anabolic = building stuff up
- Androgenic = masculine characteristics
- A class of drug — multiple types, taken in many ways
- All essentially do what testosterone does but in overdrive
- Now called iPEDs (image and performance enhancing drugs — "image" is the key word: people want to look good)
- Millions of people using currently — mostly men 20s-30s, but also women and older men
- Illegal in most sports, doctors won't prescribe → underground network with associated risks (unknown product quality, needle sharing, lack of education)

## Organ System Effects

### 1. Blood (Full Blood Count)

Steroids stimulate bone marrow to produce more red blood cells → thicker blood.

**Monitor:** Hemoglobin and Hematocrit
- If rises to dangerous levels → stop and see a doctor
- Thick blood + exercise (Valsalva during squats/deadlifts) → forced blood into heart and brain → hemorrhagic stroke risk
- Thick blood prone to clotting → embolic stroke or heart attack risk
- Dehydration/hangover makes blood even thicker

### 2. Kidney Function

Not directly affected, but indirectly:
- More muscle = more muscle breakdown = kidneys must clear that waste
- Kidneys can get overwhelmed
- **Stay hydrated with water** (not protein shakes)

### 3. Liver Function

Some steroids (especially oral/17-alpha alkylated) are toxic to the liver.

**Monitor:** ALT and Gamma GT — if rising high = toxicity → stop.

### 4. Cholesterol

Steroids massively disrupt cholesterol regulation:
- Bad cholesterol (LDL) goes up
- Good cholesterol (HDL) pushed way down
- Makes it harder to clear fat from tissues and blood vessels → blockage → heart attack
- Fatty deposit changes are often **permanent** — may not kill you now but increases heart attack risk years later

### 5. Gynecomastia / Breast Changes

- Testosterone aromatizes to estrogen → can cause fat deposits in breast tissue ("man boobs" / gynecomastia)
- In women: atrophy of breast tissue

### 6. Hair, Skin, Voice

- Increased body/facial hair growth (women too)
- Accelerated male-pattern baldness
- Acne
- Voice deepening (especially in women)

### 7. Mental Health / Psychology

Effects vary per person:
- Libido changes (up or down)
- Mania
- Irritability / "Roid Rage"
- Depression
- Self-destructive impulses (gambling, etc.)
- Body dysmorphic disorder (often overlooked, especially in men)
- **Withdrawal symptoms** when stopping: libido issues, mood changes

### 8. Hormonal (HPTA — Hypothalamic-Pituitary-Testicular Axis)

Natural testosterone feedback loop: Testosterone ↓ → LH/FSH ↑ → testes produce more test → T rises → LH/FSH suppressed.

When you take exogenous steroids:
- High testosterone completely suppresses LH and FSH
- Testes stop producing testosterone (they get the signal they're not needed)
- Testicle shrinkage
- **Coming off:** Hope is LH/FSH restart → natural test recovers. This can take **up to 6 months**
- If on too long or too high a dose → "burns your balls out" — may permanently lose natural testosterone production → **lifelong TRT from doctor**
- This also impacts **fertility** — may not think about it in your 20s but could matter later

**High testosterone → high estrogen:**
- Can cause man boobs (see above)
- Some take anti-estrogen drugs (breast cancer drugs) → even more liver/organ damage — bad idea

## Take-Home Messages for Harm Reduction

1. **Don't needle share** — goes without saying, but many do

2. **Test yourself regularly** — full panel blood tests every ~3 months minimum:
   - Baseline test (before starting)
   - Peak test (mid-cycle)
   - Recovery test (post-cycle)
   - Tracks hemoglobin, hematocrit, liver enzymes (ALT, GGT), LDL/HDL, LH/FSH, testosterone, estrogen
   - Yes it costs money — factor it into your budget

3. **Find steroid-knowledgeable doctors** — standard GP will just say "stop." Find one who understands AAS. Share full information so they can help you properly.

4. **If bloodwork shows something bad → stop** — even if you don't want to

5. **Educate yourself** — read peer-reviewed articles, don't just rely on the big guy at the gym or a TikTok influencer

6. **Lifestyle factors matter** — hydration, sleep, diet, blood pressure, weight, avoid smoking/drinking/poor sleep (all exacerbate steroid side effects). Don't forget cardio.

7. **Tell someone you trust** — mental health issues are real on steroids; body/muscle dysmorphia is real. Don't bottle it up.

## Key Bloodwork Panel When Using AAS

- Full blood count (hemoglobin, hematocrit)
- Liver function (ALT, GGT)
- Lipids (LDL, HDL)
- Kidney function (creatinine, eGFR)
- Hormones (testosterone, estrogen/E2, LH, FSH)
- PSA (prostate)
- Glucose
- Frequency: baseline → peak → recovery; or every 3 months if ongoing
