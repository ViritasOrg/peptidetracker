## Origins of AAS

- Testosterone has been around nearly 100 years (synthesized ~1930s-40s)
- Original clinical goal: create compounds that maximize anabolic outcomes with minimal androgenic outcomes — tissue-selective muscle preservation agents for:
  - Muscle wasting diseases
  - Burn patients (can't give testosterone to female children)
  - Androgen-sensitive individuals requiring anti-catabolic support
- **They never fully succeeded** in perfectly separating anabolic from androgenic activity
- This race spawned an array of pharmaceutical compounds now used in bodybuilding

## Anabolic:Androgenic Ratio Scale (Derek's Framework)

Scale of 1-10 where **5 = Testosterone** (roughly equal anabolic and androgenic in rodent studies):
- **1 (most androgenic)** → DHT
- **5 (balanced)** → Testosterone
- **10 (most anabolic)** → Primobolan (metenolone)
- Anavar also close to the anabolic end
- SARMs would be even further toward 10 if included

Note: Side effects considered "androgenic" (hair loss, acne, virilization) come from the androgenic side of the ratio.

## Key Compounds & History

### Testosterone
- Base for virtually all AAS
- ~100 years old
- Aromatizes to estrogen; DHT via 5-alpha reductase
- Foundation of every cycle

### Dianabol (Methandrostenolone / DBol)
- One of the first testosterone derivatives
- "The Breakfast of Champions" — Arnold's famous quote
- **Heavily water-retentive** → cushions joints during heavy lifting; useful for leverages
- Aromatizes significantly → estrogenic side effects

### Boldenone (Equipoise / EQ)
- Testosterone derivative
- Originally prescribed to horses, never approved for human use
- Still commonly used in bodybuilding

### Halotestin (Fluoxymesterone)
- Early compound, notably one US president was allegedly on an aggressive dose
- Protocols from that era "made almost no sense"

### Primobolan (Metenolone)
- DHT derivative → NOT a substrate for aromatase → does not convert to estrogen
- Does NOT 5-alpha reduce into problematic metabolites (more predictable)
- Most anabolic relative to androgenic of traditional AAS
- "Pure protein accretion compound" with relatively less androgenic burden
- Side effects: no water retention, very dry, very specific in action
- Used extensively by bodybuilders wanting lean, dry gains without estrogen management complexity

### Anavar (Oxandrolone)
- DHT derivative
- One of the more refined, recent compounds (though still decades old)
- Highly prized among athletes, particularly women (minimal virilization)
- Close to Primobolan on the anabolic end of the scale

### Proviron (Mesterolone)
- One of the most potent compounds at binding SHBG (sex hormone binding globulin)
- Still used to interact with SHBG to free up testosterone

### Nandrolone (Deca-Durabolin / NPP)
- Unique pharmacology: 5-alpha reduces to **dihydronandrolone** (almost no androgenic activity)
- In muscle (less 5-AR present): retains full anabolic properties
- In scalp (high 5-AR): converts to dihydronandrolone → almost no androgenic activity there either
- Result: **muscle building WITHOUT hair loss** — unique among all steroids
- Deca = long ester (side effects linger weeks); NPP = short ester (clears 3-5 days)

### Trenbolone (Tren)
- Classified as a "steroidal SARM"
- Prescribed to women in the 1980s; used to beef up cattle (may still be)
- Super anabolic but with **progestogenic activity** (interacts with progesterone receptor)
- Side effects: severe night sweats ("Tren sweats"), "Tren cough" (severe coughing fit seconds after injection)
- More suppressive — negative feedback through AR, ER, AND PR receptors
- Compounding pharmacies have made it (some prescriptions exist — "I'm prescribed Tren")

### Masteron (Drostanolone)
- DHT derivative, doesn't aromatize
- Actually **antagonizes** estrogen conversion from testosterone to some extent
- Drying effect → preferred pre-contest
- Pseudo-AI effects (like Primobolan and EQ in some individuals)

## SARMs (Selective Androgen Receptor Modulators)

- Evolved from tweaking anti-androgens to create tissue-selective AR interaction
- Goal: pure anabolic activity with no androgenic effects
- **More proportionally selective** than traditional AAS
- **But:** ceiling of anabolic activity is lower than traditional AAS
- At high doses → lose selectivity → broader spectrum effects
- **Most common:**
  - LGD-4033 (Ligandrol / VK5211) — phase 2 trial for hip fractures
  - Ostarine (Enobosarm) — most well-known, not FDA approved; popular with women competing naturally who want to bridge without virilization
- Available via underground labs and some compounding pharmacies
- All banned in natural bodybuilding, highly detectable

## Key Pharmacological Concepts

### Aromatization
Testosterone → Estrogen via aromatase enzyme. Estrogen:
- Cardioprotective, neuroprotective
- Increases strength
- Increases sex drive
- Increases anabolism *in presence of androgens*
- Causes water retention and potential gynecomastia at high levels

### 5-Alpha Reduction
Testosterone → DHT via 5-alpha reductase.
- DHT: most androgenic, responsible for hair loss/prostate effects
- Nandrolone takes a unique path (5-AR reduces to dihydronandrolone → minimal androgenic activity)

### SHBG (Sex Hormone Binding Globulin)
- Binds to sex hormones, making them unavailable
- Proviron is most potent at binding SHBG → frees up testosterone
- Mesterolone

### Estrogen Management Options
1. **AIs (Aromatase Inhibitors)** — effective but neurotoxic/cardiovascularly toxic; used in breast cancer treatment; should be last resort (few weeks pre-contest only)
2. **SERMs** — selective estrogen receptor modulators (Tamoxifen/Clomid); used for PCT or gynecomastia prevention
3. **Ratio management** — use non-aromatizing compounds (Primo, Masteron) to dilute total estrogenic load while maintaining anabolism (modern "safer use" approach)

## The "Safer Use" Model (Evidence-Based Approach)

Championed by people like Joe Jeffrey (UK). Core principles:
- Manage estrogen through compound selection ratios, NOT AIs
- Use minimum effective doses
- Monitor bloodwork obsessively
- Understand each compound's individual mechanism before adding it
- Accept that individual response varies enormously (some people tolerate high estrogen fine, others don't)
